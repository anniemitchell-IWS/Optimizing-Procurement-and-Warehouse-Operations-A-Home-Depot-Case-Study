# Executive Summary

## Overview

The Home Depot has continued to strengthen its position in the professional (Pro) market through strategic acquisitions, digital transformation, and investments in its supply chain. The acquisitions of SRS Distribution in 2024 and GMS in 2025 significantly expanded the company's capabilities across specialty building materials, allowing it to better serve professional contractors throughout the entire project lifecycle.

As Home Depot continues integrating these businesses, procurement, warehouse operations, supplier management, inventory visibility, and fulfillment become increasingly interconnected. Coordinating these functions efficiently is essential to supporting continued growth while maintaining operational excellence.

This independent business case study analyzes Home Depot's publicly available business strategy to identify opportunities for continued process optimization across procurement and warehouse operations. Rather than evaluating Home Depot's internal systems or business decisions, this project demonstrates how business process analysis, enterprise resource planning (ERP) concepts, and process improvement methodologies can support a growing enterprise.

---

## Business Objective

The objective of this case study is to explore how procurement and warehouse operations can continue supporting Home Depot's expanding Pro Supply Network through improved process integration, operational visibility, and cross-functional collaboration.

---

## Project Goals

This project seeks to:

- Analyze Home Depot's publicly available Pro business strategy.
- Examine procurement and warehouse operations from a business process perspective.
- Identify opportunities for process optimization using publicly available information.
- Apply ERP concepts to support procurement, inventory, and warehouse operations.
- Recommend measurable operational improvements through key performance indicators (KPIs).

---

## Key Focus Areas

- Procurement Strategy
- Supplier Management
- Warehouse Operations
- Inventory Visibility
- Process Integration
- Operational Efficiency
- Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)
- Business Process Improvement

---

## Scope

This case study is based exclusively on publicly available information, including Home Depot press releases, SEC filings, investor communications, and other publicly accessible resources. All observations, analyses, and recommendations represent an independent academic and professional exercise and should not be interpreted as representing Home Depot's internal operations or strategic decisions.

---

## Expected Outcome

The purpose of this project is not to redesign Home Depot's operations, but to demonstrate the application of business analysis, process improvement, and enterprise operations principles within the context of a Fortune 500 organization undergoing continued growth and transformation.
