# Optimizing-Procurement-and-Warehouse-Operations
---

##  Project Overview

Home Depot has made significant investments in expanding its Pro business through strategic acquisitions, supply chain modernization, and enhanced fulfillment capabilities. As its network continues to grow, integrating procurement, warehouse operations, inventory visibility, and fulfillment processes becomes increasingly important to delivering a seamless experience for professional contractors.

This independent case study examines publicly available information surrounding Home Depot's Pro Supply Network and explores opportunities to optimize procurement and warehouse operations through business process improvement and Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) concepts. Rather than evaluating Home Depot's internal systems, this project demonstrates how structured analysis and process improvement methodologies can be applied to a real-world business scenario.

The recommendations presented throughout this repository are based on publicly available information, industry best practices, and ERP principles. They are intended solely for educational and portfolio purposes.

---

##  Business Problem

The expansion of Home Depot's Pro business—including the acquisitions of SRS Distribution and GMS—has increased the complexity of procurement, supplier management, warehouse operations, inventory coordination, and fulfillment across its supply network.

As organizations grow through acquisitions and expand their distribution capabilities, operational processes must evolve to maintain visibility, efficiency, and consistency across multiple business units. This case study explores how process optimization can support those objectives while improving operational performance.

---

##  Project Objectives

This project aims to:

- Analyze Home Depot's publicly available supply chain strategy and Pro business expansion.
- Examine procurement and warehouse management processes from an operational perspective.
- Identify potential opportunities for process improvement across the procure-to-fulfillment lifecycle.
- Apply Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) concepts to support business process optimization.
- Develop measurable Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to evaluate proposed improvements.
- Demonstrate analytical thinking, process mapping, and business communication skills.

##  Methodology

This case study follows a structured business analysis approach:

1. Research publicly available information.
2. Understand Home Depot's business strategy.
3. Map current operational processes.
4. Identify potential operational bottlenecks.
5. Develop process improvement recommendations.
6. Apply ERP concepts to proposed solutions.
7. Measure success through operational KPIs.
