# KPI Framework

## Overview

To evaluate the effectiveness of procurement, warehouse operations, and enterprise integration initiatives, organizations rely on Key Performance Indicators (KPIs). These metrics provide measurable insights into operational performance and help leadership identify opportunities for continuous improvement.

The following KPIs align with the recommendations presented in this case study and support Home Depot's publicly stated focus on operational excellence, supply chain modernization, and serving professional customers.

---

## Procurement KPIs

| KPI | Purpose | Business Value |
|------|---------|----------------|
| Purchase Order Cycle Time | Measures the time required to process a purchase order. | Improves procurement efficiency and responsiveness. |
| Supplier On-Time Delivery | Measures the percentage of supplier deliveries received on schedule. | Supports product availability and customer satisfaction. |
| Procurement Cost Savings | Tracks cost reductions achieved through strategic sourcing and supplier partnerships. | Improves purchasing efficiency and profitability. |

---

## Warehouse KPIs

| KPI | Purpose | Business Value |
|------|---------|----------------|
| Inventory Accuracy | Measures the accuracy of recorded inventory compared to physical inventory. | Improves inventory visibility and reduces stock discrepancies. |
| Inventory Turnover | Measures how efficiently inventory is sold and replenished. | Reduces excess inventory while maintaining product availability. |
| Warehouse Utilization | Measures how efficiently warehouse space is being used. | Supports operational efficiency and capacity planning. |

---

## Fulfillment KPIs

| KPI | Purpose | Business Value |
|------|---------|----------------|
| Order Fill Rate | Measures the percentage of customer orders fulfilled without shortages. | Improves customer satisfaction and operational reliability. |
| Perfect Order Percentage | Measures orders delivered complete, on time, and without errors. | Reflects overall fulfillment performance. |
| Order Processing Time | Measures the time required to prepare and ship customer orders. | Supports faster delivery and improved service levels. |

---

## Enterprise Integration KPIs

| KPI | Purpose | Business Value |
|------|---------|----------------|
| Cross-Business Inventory Visibility | Measures inventory transparency across Home Depot, SRS Distribution, and GMS. | Supports informed decision-making and inventory optimization. |
| Supplier Performance Score | Evaluates supplier reliability, quality, and delivery performance. | Strengthens supplier relationships and procurement decisions. |
| Inventory Transfer Efficiency | Measures how effectively inventory moves across the Pro Supply Network. | Improves collaboration between business units and reduces stock shortages. |

---

## KPI Summary

Monitoring these KPIs enables organizations to evaluate operational performance, identify process improvement opportunities, and measure progress toward strategic business objectives.

Together, these metrics support continuous improvement across procurement, warehouse operations, inventory management, and fulfillment while reinforcing Home Depot's long-term strategy of delivering an integrated experience for professional contractors.
